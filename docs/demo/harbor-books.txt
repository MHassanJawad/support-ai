Harbor Books - Customer Support Policy

Business hours
Harbor Books opens Monday to Saturday from 9 AM to 7 PM and is closed on Sundays.

Returns
Unread books in their original condition may be returned within 14 days of purchase.
Customers must bring the original receipt. Refunds use the original payment method.
Personalized books cannot be returned unless damaged on arrival.

Delivery
Delivery within Lahore costs PKR 250 per order and takes 2 to 3 business days.
Orders above PKR 5000 qualify for free delivery within Lahore.

Contact
For damaged deliveries, contact support within 48 hours of receipt.
